
# 🧭 Training Systems Approach

> **I don't treat training as a collection of courses. I look at how people, processes, information, technology, and learning interact—and use that understanding to design better training operations.**

---

## What This Portfolio Shows

This repository presents the way I think about training as a **system rather than a collection of courses**.

I start with the work, examine the relationships around learning, and then determine what kind of intervention is actually useful.

The approach connects:

**People · Processes · Information · Technology · Learning**

The objective is simple:

> **Understand the work. Identify what is getting in the way. Decide where learning can help. Design around the task. Observe what happens. Improve.**

---

## 🎯 A Practical Philosophy

I don't begin with:

> **"Which tool should I use?"**

I begin with:

> **"What is happening, what needs to happen, and what is getting in the way?"**

From there, I determine whether the right response is learning content, practice, digital support, documentation, process improvement, or a combination.

## 🔐 About the Portfolio Evidence

This repository is intended to demonstrate **how I think and work**, not to disclose confidential organizational information.

Examples and demonstrations should therefore use:

- publicly available information
- original portfolio work

I do not publish confidential client, employer, learner, or organizational information.
